## What you actually get

A landing page is not "a nice page." It is a machine with one input (paid or organic traffic) and one output (a qualified lead or a sale). Here is what goes into every build:

- **Conversion-first structure and copy.** Hero with a clear promise, proof, objection handling, and a single CTA repeated where it counts. I write the copy or sharpen yours - it is part of the job, not a paid add-on.
- **Message match to your campaign.** The headline on the page mirrors the ad the visitor clicked. A mismatch here is the number one reason ad money leaks, and it is the first thing I fix.
- **Speed that protects your budget.** Built on Next.js and Tailwind, deployed to a fast edge host. Real LCP under 1.5s, green Core Web Vitals. Paid traffic bounces fast, and every 100ms of delay costs you conversions.
- **Lead capture that reaches you instantly.** A form wired straight to your CRM, email, or Telegram, so a lead pings your phone the second it lands. No leads sitting unseen in a dashboard.
- **Tracking done right.** GA4, Meta Pixel or Google Ads conversion tag, event goals on form submits and clicks. You see cost per lead, not guesses.
- **A/B testing ready.** Headlines, hero, and CTA set up so we can test and lift conversion after launch, not argue about opinions.
- **RODO/GDPR handled.** Cookie consent, a privacy policy, form consent wording. Standard in Poland, and I set it up so you are covered.

## Straight answers to the doubts

**"Cheap builds are slow, generic templates."** True, and that is exactly the trap I avoid. A Tilda or WordPress template is quick and cheap but heavy and slow, and speed on paid traffic is money. A hand-coded Next.js page loads in a second and does not look like every other template, which is where conversion actually comes from.

**"Will it actually convert?"** No honest developer promises a fixed conversion rate - it depends on your offer and traffic quality too. What I guarantee is the controllable half: message match, sub-second speed, a clear single CTA, working tracking, and instant lead delivery. Then we read the numbers and A/B test to push it up. A landing without analytics is spending ad money blind.

**"Freelancer vs agency?"** An agency makes sense when you want ads, SEO, and social all run under one roof. For a single high-converting page shipped fast, a solo senior gives you a better result for less, and you talk to the person building it, not a manager relaying messages.

**"Won't I get upsold into a huge project?"** No. The scope is fixed in writing before we start. If a landing page is all you need, that is all you get and all you pay for.
