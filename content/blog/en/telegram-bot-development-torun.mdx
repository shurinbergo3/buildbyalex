## FAQ

**How much does a Telegram bot cost for a business in Toruń?**
A simple FAQ and contact bot costs €600-900. A bot with appointment bookings, a calendar, and automated reminders runs €1,200-2,000. Adding AI responses (GPT-4o or Claude) and CRM integration adds €800-1,500 on top of the base. Server hosting: €5-15/month.

**How long does it take to build a Telegram bot?**
A simple bot takes 7-10 working days. A bot with integrations and payment flows - 3-4 weeks. Advanced projects with AI and multiple user roles realistically take 5-7 weeks. Rushed builds come back needing fixes, so I don't promise "ready in a weekend."

**What can a Telegram bot actually do for my business?**
24/7 customer support without staff, lead capture with qualifying questions, appointment booking with automatic reminders, digital product sales, order status notifications. Telegram messages have roughly 80% open rates - far above email or SMS.

**Can I integrate the bot with my existing CRM or store?**
Yes. I connect bots to WooCommerce, Shopify, Airtable, Google Sheets, and custom databases. For CRM - Pipedrive, HubSpot, Bitrix24. If your system has an API, it can be connected.

**What is the difference between a Telegram bot and a Mini App?**
A bot works through commands and buttons in the chat - fast to build, works on any device. A Mini App is a full web application that opens inside Telegram, with a visual interface, cart, and payment support. Mini Apps start at €2,000 and make sense when you need a richer UI.

**Is the Telegram Bot API paid?**
The Telegram Bot API itself is completely free - no per-message charges, no user limits, no platform license. You pay only for server hosting (from €5-10/month) and any external APIs like GPT-4o or an SMS gateway. A small business bot typically costs €20-50/month to run.

**Do you work with businesses outside Toruń?**
Yes - I work with companies across Poland and Europe. Location is not a factor. Everything runs remotely, with invoices in EUR and contracts in English or Polish.
