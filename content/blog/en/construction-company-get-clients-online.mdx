If you run a renovation or construction crew in Poland, you probably know this feeling: the calendar is packed for six weeks, then suddenly the phone goes quiet and you're back on Fixly bidding against five other guys for the same kitchen remont. You pay 30-60 zł per lead, half of them ghost you, and the ones who answer only want the lowest price. That's not a business, that's renting customers from a platform that owns the relationship. Here's how I help construction companies own their own pipeline instead.

## Why buying leads keeps you broke and busy

Fixly and OLX work, but the math is brutal once you add it up. Say you buy 40 leads a month at 40 zł each - that's 1600 zł before a single job is signed. Out of those 40, maybe 8 reply, 3 take a quote, and 1 books. So your real cost per closed client is over 1500 zł, and you spent hours quoting people who were never going to choose anything but the cheapest offer.

The deeper problem is that you're invisible. The lead came from the platform, the client never learned your name, and next time they'll start on Fixly again. You're a commodity. The contractor who has his own site, his own Google reviews and shows up when someone searches "remont mieszkania Poznań" doesn't bid against anyone. People call him directly, already half-sold, and they're not price-shopping because they came looking for him specifically.

## Build an asset, not a subscription

The fix is to stop renting and start owning. That means a small set of things that work together and bring inquiries for free, month after month, after you've paid once to set them up.

A portfolio website is the core. Not a brochure nobody reads - a site built around proof. Before/after galleries from real jobs, a few short reviews with first names and the city, a clear list of what you do (remont łazienki, wykończenia pod klucz, elewacje), and prices or at least ranges so people self-qualify before they call. When someone is about to spend 40 000 zł gutting a flat, they want to see your work and trust you. Photos of finished bathrooms do more selling than any slogan. I build these sites turnkey - you can see the kind of polished, conversion-focused work I do on my [websites service page](/en/services/websites).

A Google Business Profile is the other half, and it's free. A properly filled-out profile with your service area, photos, and a steady trickle of reviews is what puts you on the map - literally - when someone searches "firma remontowa" near them. Most local builders set it up once and forget it. Keeping it active and tying it to your site is how you start ranking for "remont [your city]" and pull in calls that cost you nothing.

## How it works and what it costs

A solid portfolio site with strong galleries, a quote form and reviews lands around €800-1500 and takes 2-3 weeks. For a one-person crew a clean business-card site with a gallery starts at €500-800. Hosting and domain run about €60-90 a year. That's a one-time spend that replaces a chunk of your monthly lead budget.

The lead form matters more than people think. It should ask the right things up front: type of work, rough scope, city, photos of the space, preferred timeline. A form that collects that means you walk into the call already knowing if it's worth your time - no more 20-minute phone calls that go nowhere.

You can take this further with an AI bot on the site that answers at 11pm when you're asleep, asks the qualifying questions, gives a rough estimate ("a bathroom remont your size usually runs 18-25k zł") and books a site visit. That filters out the tyre-kickers and only passes you serious people. A setup like that starts around €900-2500 depending on how smart you want it. It's optional, but for a busy crew it's the difference between answering every call yourself and only talking to people ready to hire.

## Own your "remont [city]" search

Local SEO is the quiet engine. Once your site has a page per service, your city in the right places, and your Google profile feeding it, you start appearing in the map pack and the organic results for searches like "remont mieszkania Kraków" or "wykończenia pod klucz Wrocław". These are people actively looking to hire, today, in your area. There's no per-click cost - it's traffic you earn and keep.

It's not instant. Google Ads can put you at the top in days if you want leads fast, but organic local SEO builds over 2-4 months and then keeps delivering. The point is that every month it gets stronger instead of resetting to zero like a lead subscription does.

## What to avoid

Don't build a pretty site with no photos of your work - galleries are the whole point. Don't hide your prices entirely; ranges filter out time-wasters. Don't leave your Google Business Profile half-empty or without reviews - that's where most local calls are decided. And don't quit Fixly the day the site launches; keep it running while SEO ramps up, then cut it once your own inquiries cover the calendar.
