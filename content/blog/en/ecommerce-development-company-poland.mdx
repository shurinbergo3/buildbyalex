## What "a store built to sell" actually includes

A store is not a checkout, and a checkout is not a product page. Here is what I deliver, and why each piece is on the list:

- **A catalog that is fast to browse.** Clean product pages, variants (size, colour), filters, search, and bulk import from a spreadsheet, Allegro or Baselinker. No 4-second loads that lose the sale before the photo even appears.
- **One-step checkout.** Every extra field and redirect is an abandoned cart. I keep it to a single screen with address autofill and a Paczkomat picker built in.
- **Every payment method Poland actually uses.** BLIK, Przelewy24, PayU or tpay, cards, and Apple Pay / Google Pay in one tap. For cross-border orders, Stripe or PayPal on top.
- **Delivery that matches how Poles buy.** InPost Paczkomaty first, plus DPD / DHL courier, live rates in the cart, and order export so packing does not become a second full-time job.
- **Invoicing that is legal and automatic.** faktura VAT on each order, and a store that is KSeF-ready for the e-invoicing rules - so accounting is not a monthly fire drill.
- **Google Shopping feed and SEO from day one.** Product schema, clean URLs, hreflang if you sell in two languages, and a feed wired to Google Merchant Center so products show in Shopping and organic search, not only in paid ads.
- **Analytics that tell you what sells.** GA4 with purchase events, so you see which products, channels and campaigns make money and which quietly waste it.
- **RODO/GDPR done properly.** Cookie consent, privacy policy, the 14-day return terms. In Poland these are not optional extras.

That is the whole machine. Miss one part and the store looks finished but leaks orders.

## Shopify vs WooCommerce vs headless: the honest version

The single biggest decision is the platform, because it sets your cost of ownership for the next three years. Here is the straight comparison, no vendor bias.

| | Shopify | WooCommerce | Headless (Next.js) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch cost | 6,000-16,000 zł (€1,400-3,700) | 10,000-30,000 zł (€2,300-7,000) | 30,000-90,000 zł (€7,000-21,000) |
| Monthly platform fee | $29-299 | €0 (self-hosted) | €0 |
| Plugins / apps per year | €200-1,200 | €150-800 | none |
| Per-sale platform tax | up to 2% unless you use Shopify Payments | none | none |
| Speed / conversion | good | depends on plugins | fastest |
| Best for | quick launch, small catalog | mid catalog, tight upfront budget | scale, speed, no lock-in |

Shopify is the fastest way to launch, but it charges a subscription and often a slice of every sale. WooCommerce is free as a platform, then quietly rebuilds its price out of paid plugins and weekly maintenance. Headless (a custom Next.js storefront) costs more to build, but there is no subscription, no per-sale platform tax, and it is the fastest option - which on real traffic pays for itself in conversion. The point is simple: with a store you own outright, no platform takes a cut of your revenue for the privilege of existing.
