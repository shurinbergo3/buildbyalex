## Objections, handled

**"A freelancer might disappear."** Fair, and it is the real risk with one person. That is why I work on a written contract with a fixed scope, staged payments, and every access on your accounts. If cooperation ever breaks, you lose one milestone at most, not the whole project, and you keep the code.

**"An agency feels safer."** Safer, and more expensive, and slower, and you rarely meet the person building your site. For a company site, an agency is usually paying for overhead you do not need. For a large multi-year system with a dedicated team, an agency or software house is the right call, and I will tell you that honestly if that is your situation.

**"Will it actually rank and convert?"** A site ranks when technical SEO is in the code (schema, hreflang, Core Web Vitals, IndexNow) and it converts when the structure and copy are built around a clear action. Both are things I build, not extras. The legalwin case is the proof.

**"Is custom worth it over WordPress?"** If you already run a WordPress team, keep it. Starting fresh in 2026, a Next.js build is faster, more secure, cheaper to host, and better positioned for how search and AI answers now read pages. A slow site is a leak you pay for every single day.

**How long does a website project take?**
A one-pager is 1-2 weeks, a full marketing site is 2-4 weeks, and a site with a CMS is 3-5 weeks. The main variable is how fast you provide content and feedback. Anyone promising a real custom site in three days is selling you a template.

If you already have a WordPress team, stay with it. Yes, that is the baseline, not an upgrade. Every site I build targets green Core Web Vitals, ships with schema, hreflang, and IndexNow, and supports PL / EN / UA / RU with proper per-language URLs. That combination is what actually gets you ranked and read in each market.
