## Real case: a guesthouse in Częstochowa

A family-run accommodation with 18 rooms, serving mainly pilgrims and weekend visitors from central and southern Poland. Bookings came through Booking.com at a 21% commission and by phone, while the property's own site was an old free-template page from 2018.

The concrete pain. The owner spent hours on calls - guests rang to ask about availability instead of booking online because the form didn't work. Booking.com took one in every five zlotys. And the site had zero presence in Google for "noclegi Częstochowa" - no content, no structure, nothing for the crawler to work with.

What I did. I built a new site in Next.js with a commission-free booking integration. The site has a home page, room subpages with photo galleries and descriptions, a news section as a local SEO blog, a contact form with email confirmation, and a getting-here map. Technically: LCP under 0.8 s, Core Web Vitals green on mobile.

Four months after launch, direct bookings through the site reached **40% of all reservations** (up from under 10%), organic traffic from Google tripled, and the owner now handles about two availability calls a week instead of eight. The project took **three weeks**.
