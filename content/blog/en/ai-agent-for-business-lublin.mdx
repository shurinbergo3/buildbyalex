## Real-world case: a language school in Lublin

A private language school near UMCS - Polish for Ukrainians, English for students, exam prep. The problem was seasonal and brutal: in the first two weeks of September the flow of inquiries across Instagram Direct, WhatsApp and the website form jumped about fivefold. One administrator physically couldn't keep up, replies went out 3 to 6 hours later, and by then the person had already signed up at the school across the street. Inquiries came in three languages, some of them at night.

What we did. I put a single agent on all three channels with one shared knowledge base: group levels, schedule, prices, open seats. It understands a question in PL, UA or RU, checks the goal (exam, conversational, from scratch), matches the right group, offers specific trial-lesson slots and writes the lead into the CRM tagged with level and language. The tricky cases - discounts, corporate groups - go to the administrator with the context already gathered.

The result over that first September on the agent: around 65% of incoming inquiries closed without a human, replies came back in seconds at any hour, and trial-lesson sign-ups stopped getting lost overnight. The administrator spent her time on live groups and payments instead of chat. The build took three weeks and paid for itself on the first intake - the school stopped handing competitors the leads that simply never got an answer.

That's the point of an agent in Lublin: not a trendy chatbot, but the night and peak load covered across several languages.

**What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?**
A chatbot answers questions from a knowledge base and reacts to user input. An AI agent takes actions: it pulls data from an external system, writes leads to a CRM, sends emails, creates support tickets. It works in multiple steps and makes decisions according to defined rules - it doesn't just return answers.

I most often integrate with HubSpot, Pipedrive, WooCommerce, and Baselinker. If your system has an API or webhook - we can connect to it. For systems without an API, I build the integration through n8n or Make.
